# Minutes of the Board of Directors Meeting

Company: Sentineth Technologies Limited
Date: Thursday 19 March 2026
Time: 14:00 to 16:35 GMT
Location: London office, Boardroom, and video conference
Chair: Sir Alistair Pemberton-Rowe, Non-Executive Chair
Company Secretary: Yusuf Adeyemi

Present: Sir Alistair Pemberton-Rowe (Chair), Helena Vasquez (Chief Executive
Officer), Priya Raghunathan (Chief Financial Officer, attending for items 2
to 5), Dr Konstantin Mirov (Non-Executive Director), Fenella Oyelaran
(Non-Executive Director, by video), Bartholomew Krieger (Investor Director,
Northgate Ventures)

In attendance: Marcus Bellweather (item 6 only), Yusuf Adeyemi (Secretary)
Apologies: None

## 1. Quorum, conflicts, and prior minutes

The Chair confirmed a quorum. No new conflicts of interest were declared.
Bartholomew Krieger reiterated his standing conflict in respect of Northgate
Ventures' holding in Corvid Logistics, a customer, and confirmed he would
abstain from any vote concerning that relationship.

The minutes of the meeting held on 22 January 2026 were approved without
amendment.

## 2. Chief Executive's report

Helena Vasquez reported that annual recurring revenue reached GBP 8.4 million
at the end of February, against a plan of GBP 8.1 million. Net revenue
retention for the trailing twelve months was 114 per cent.

Two enterprise contracts signed in the quarter: Halloway Retail Group at GBP
420,000 annually on a three-year term, and a second contract subject to a
confidentiality restriction, at GBP 310,000 annually on a two-year term.

Gross churn was 6.2 per cent annualised, concentrated in the self-service
tier. Helena noted that no enterprise customer has churned in eighteen months.

Headcount stood at sixty-one at month end against a plan of sixty-four. The
shortfall is entirely in engineering and reflects a deliberate decision to
hold out for senior candidates rather than fill at mid level.

## 3. Financial review

Priya Raghunathan presented the management accounts to 28 February 2026.

Cash at bank was GBP 11.2 million. Monthly net burn averaged GBP 640,000 over
the trailing three months, giving a runway of seventeen months on the current
plan, or twenty-one months under the downside scenario in which no further
hiring occurs.

Gross margin was 71 per cent, down from 74 per cent in the prior quarter. The
decline is attributed to increased inference and storage costs associated with
the two new enterprise contracts, which carry higher document volumes than the
pricing model assumed.

The Board discussed the gross margin trajectory at some length. Dr Mirov asked
whether the pricing model needs revision before further enterprise contracts
are signed. Priya undertook to bring a revised unit economics analysis to the
May meeting, including a per-document cost breakdown.

Action: Priya Raghunathan to present revised unit economics, including
inference cost per document ingested, at the May Board meeting.

## 4. Fundraising

The Board discussed the timing of a Series B. Bartholomew Krieger advised that
the current market rewards demonstrated retention over growth rate, and that
the 114 per cent net revenue retention figure is the strongest element of the
story.

The Board agreed not to open a process before September 2026, on the basis
that two further quarters of retention data materially improves the position
and that runway does not compel an earlier start.

Resolution: the Board approved in principle the appointment of a financial
adviser for a Series B process, with the appointment to be brought back for
approval no later than the July meeting. Carried unanimously.

## 5. Customer concentration

The Board reviewed customer concentration. The largest customer represents 9.1
per cent of annual recurring revenue and the top five represent 27 per cent.
The Board noted this is within the tolerance set in the risk register, which
sets a threshold of 15 per cent for any single customer.

Priya Raghunathan left the meeting at 15:40.

## 6. Information security update

Marcus Bellweather presented the annual security report. The company holds a
current ISO 27001 certificate, renewed in November 2025 with two minor
non-conformities, both closed.

There were no reportable personal data breaches in the period. One security
incident of severity two was recorded, INC-2026-0119, involving a misconfigured
storage bucket that was publicly readable for nine hours. Access logs
confirmed no external access occurred. The Board accepted the assessment that
notification was not required.

Dr Mirov asked about the company's exposure to a supply chain compromise.
Marcus described the vendor review process and acknowledged that the current
process reviews vendors at onboarding and annually, but does not continuously
monitor for changes in vendor security posture between reviews.

Action: Marcus Bellweather to propose a continuous vendor monitoring approach,
with costs, for the July meeting.

## 7. Any other business and close

The Chair noted that Fenella Oyelaran's initial three-year term expires in
October 2026 and invited the Nomination Committee to bring a recommendation on
renewal to the July meeting.

There being no further business, the Chair closed the meeting at 16:35. The
next meeting is scheduled for Thursday 21 May 2026 at 14:00.
