# Travel and Expense Policy

Owner: Priya Raghunathan, Head of Finance
Version: 4.2
Effective date: 1 February 2026
Supersedes: Version 4.1 (issued 3 March 2025)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs all business travel and all reimbursable expenditure
incurred by employees, contractors, and interns of Sentineth Technologies
Limited. It applies to every legal entity in the group, including the UK
parent and the Indian subsidiary, Sentineth Technologies India Private
Limited. Where local statutory requirements conflict with this policy, the
local requirement takes precedence and the Finance team must be notified in
writing within five working days.

Contractors engaged through an agency are covered by the terms of their
statement of work rather than by this policy, unless the statement of work
explicitly incorporates this document by reference.

## 2. Approval thresholds

All travel must be approved before booking. There are no exceptions to
advance approval, including for travel booked at short notice during an
incident response.

Domestic travel under GBP 500 in total cost requires approval from the
employee's direct line manager only. Domestic travel at or above GBP 500
requires approval from a department head. International travel of any value
requires approval from a department head and countersignature from Finance.
Any single trip with a projected total cost above GBP 4,000 requires written
approval from the Chief Financial Officer before any booking is made.

Approvals are recorded in the Expense Portal and expire after sixty days. A
lapsed approval must be re-obtained; it cannot be extended by email.

## 3. Air travel

Economy class is the default for all flights with a scheduled duration under
six hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights between six and nine
hours. Business class may be booked only for flights exceeding nine hours in
a single leg, and only with department head approval obtained separately from
the general travel approval.

Employees must book through the corporate travel desk operated by Wexford
Travel Management. Direct bookings through consumer websites are reimbursable
only where the corporate desk was unavailable for more than four hours and
the employee has documented the attempt.

Seat selection fees are reimbursable up to GBP 45 per leg. Checked baggage is
reimbursable for one bag per journey. Airline lounge access is not
reimbursable except where the scheduled connection time exceeds four hours.

## 4. Accommodation

Hotel accommodation is reimbursed at actual cost subject to nightly caps. The
cap is GBP 180 per night in London, GBP 140 per night elsewhere in the United
Kingdom, GBP 210 per night in New York and San Francisco, and GBP 120 per
night in all other locations. Caps are exclusive of local occupancy taxes but
inclusive of breakfast where the rate bundles it.

Where no compliant room is available within the cap, the employee may book up
to twenty per cent above the cap provided a screenshot of the search results
is attached to the expense claim. Bookings more than twenty per cent above
cap require prior Finance approval.

Short-term rental accommodation is permitted for stays of seven nights or
longer where it reduces total cost. Employees remain responsible for ensuring
the property meets the company's duty-of-care standards.

## 5. Meals and subsistence

Meals are reimbursed up to GBP 40 per day for domestic travel and GBP 65 per
day for international travel. These are daily aggregate limits, not per-meal
limits, and unused allowance does not carry forward between days.

Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances when claimed as a
personal meal expense. Alcohol served as part of a client entertainment event
is reimbursable within the entertainment limits set out in section 6.

Where a meal is provided as part of a conference registration or by a client,
the daily allowance is reduced proportionally: by GBP 8 for a provided
breakfast, GBP 14 for a provided lunch, and GBP 18 for a provided dinner.

## 6. Client entertainment

Client entertainment requires pre-approval from a department head and is
capped at GBP 90 per attendee including the host. The names and employers of
all attendees must be recorded on the claim. Entertainment of public
officials is prohibited without written clearance from the General Counsel,
regardless of value.

## 7. Ground transport and mileage

The mileage rate for private vehicles is 45 pence per mile for the first
10,000 miles in a tax year and 25 pence per mile thereafter, consistent with
HMRC approved mileage allowance payments. Passengers carried on company
business may be claimed at an additional 5 pence per mile per passenger.

Taxi and ride-hailing journeys are reimbursable where public transport is
unavailable, where the journey occurs between 22:00 and 06:00, or where the
employee is carrying equipment weighing more than ten kilograms. Rail travel
should be booked in standard class; first class is reimbursable only where
the advance-purchase first class fare is lower than the standard walk-up fare.

## 8. Submission and deadlines

Expense claims must be submitted within thirty days of the expenditure being
incurred. Claims submitted between thirty-one and sixty days require a written
explanation and department head approval. Claims submitted more than sixty
days after the expenditure will not be reimbursed except in cases of extended
medical leave.

Receipts are required for every claimed item above GBP 15. A card statement
is not an acceptable substitute for an itemised receipt. Missing receipt
declarations are permitted no more than three times per employee per
financial year.

Reimbursement is paid through the payroll cycle following claim approval.
Employees should expect payment within twenty-five working days of approval.

## 9. Non-compliance

Deliberate submission of a false or inflated claim is treated as gross
misconduct and may result in summary dismissal. Repeated inadvertent breaches
of this policy will be addressed through the standard performance management
process. Finance conducts a random audit of five per cent of all claims each
quarter, and a full audit of any employee whose claims exceed GBP 20,000 in a
rolling twelve-month period.
