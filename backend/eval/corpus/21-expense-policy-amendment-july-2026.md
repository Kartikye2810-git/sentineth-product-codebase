# Finance Notice: Amendments to the Travel and Expense Policy

Issued by: Priya Raghunathan, Head of Finance
Issue date: 1 July 2026
Effective date: 1 August 2026
Applies to: Travel and Expense Policy version 4.2

## Why this notice exists

Three limits in the current policy have drifted away from what they were set
against. Two of them are now materially below market and are producing
compliant claims that Finance approves by exception every month, which is a
sign the limit is wrong rather than that the claimants are. The third is
being used more often than it was designed for.

Rather than reissue the whole policy for three figures, this notice amends
them directly. It will be folded into version 4.3, which is scheduled for
January 2027. Until then this notice and the policy are read together, and
where they differ, this notice is the operative figure.

## Amendment 1: London accommodation

The London nightly accommodation limit is raised to GBP 205. The figure in
section 4 of the policy was set in early 2025 and has been exceeded by the
twenty per cent tolerance in a third of London bookings since April.

No other city limit changes. The tolerance for bookings above the limit, the
evidence required to use it, and the requirement for prior Finance approval
beyond the tolerance are all unchanged.

## Amendment 2: Client entertainment

The client entertainment limit is reduced to GBP 75 per attendee, host
included. This follows the audit committee's review of entertainment spend in
the first half of the year and applies to events booked on or after the
effective date. Events already booked at the previous limit may proceed.

The requirement to record every attendee and their employer is unchanged, as
is the prohibition on entertaining public officials without clearance from
the General Counsel. Neither is affected by the change in value.

## Amendment 3: Missing receipt declarations

An employee may now use the missing receipt declaration at most twice in a
financial year. The declaration was introduced for the genuinely lost
receipt, and the current allowance is being used as a routine substitute for
keeping one. Usage is reported to department heads quarterly from August.

Employees who have already made two declarations in the current financial
year should expect the third to be refused rather than approved late.

## What has not changed

The approval thresholds in section 2, the air travel classes in section 3,
the meal allowances in section 5, the mileage rates in section 7, and the
submission deadlines in section 8 are all unchanged by this notice. Any
figure not named above should be taken from the policy itself.

## Questions

Finance Operations holds the answers to process questions arising from this
notice. Questions about whether a specific booking falls before or after the
effective date should be raised before the booking is made, not with the
claim afterwards.
