# Expenses: Frequently Asked Questions

Maintained by: Finance Operations
Companion to: the Travel and Expense Policy, which is authoritative
Last updated: 12 June 2026

This page answers process questions about using the Expense Portal. Where a
question concerns what may be claimed or the applicable limit, the Travel and
Expense Policy is the authority and this page deliberately does not restate
its figures, so that the two cannot drift apart.

## Getting access

How do I get into the Expense Portal? Access is provisioned automatically on
your first day through the identity provider. If you cannot sign in, the cause
is almost always that your start date in the HR system is in the future.

Can a colleague submit on my behalf? No. Claims are submitted by the person
who incurred the cost. A manager may approve, but never submit.

I am a contractor. Do I use this system? Only if your statement of work says
so. Most agency contractors invoice through their agency instead.

## Submitting a claim

What format do receipts need to be? A photograph is fine provided the vendor,
the date, the total, and the itemised lines are all legible. Screenshots of
card statements are not accepted, because they do not itemise.

The receipt is in a foreign currency. What rate applies? The portal converts
at the rate on the transaction date automatically. Do not convert manually;
manual conversion is the single most common cause of a returned claim.

Can I submit several trips together? Yes, but keep one claim per trip. Mixed
claims are harder to approve and are the second most common cause of a return.

What if I lost the receipt? Use the missing receipt declaration in the portal.
The policy limits how often you may do this in a year.

## Approvals

Who approves my claim? Your line manager by default. The portal routes
automatically based on the claim value and type, so you do not need to choose.

My manager is on leave. Their delegate approves. Delegates are configured in
the portal by the manager before leave; if none was set, contact Finance
Operations and we will reassign.

How long does approval take? Most claims are approved within three working
days. Claims routed to Finance for secondary review typically take longer.

## Payment

When will I be paid? Reimbursement is made through the payroll cycle following
approval. The exact timing is stated in the policy.

Can I be paid before the trip? Yes, for pre-booked travel where the cost falls
on you personally. Raise a prepayment request rather than an expense claim.

Why was my claim reduced rather than rejected? Where part of a claim is
compliant and part is not, Finance approves the compliant part and annotates
the rest. The annotation explains which item was reduced and why.

## Corrections and disputes

I made a mistake on a submitted claim. If it has not been approved, withdraw
and resubmit it. If it has been approved but not paid, contact Finance
Operations. If it has been paid, the correction is handled through payroll.

I disagree with a rejection. Reply on the claim first, since most rejections
are a misunderstanding about evidence. If it remains unresolved, escalate to
your department head, who may take it to Finance.

## Corporate cards

Who gets a corporate card? Employees who travel more than four times a year,
on request from their department head.

Do card transactions still need a claim? Yes. Every card transaction must be
reconciled with a receipt in the portal within the same deadline that applies
to reimbursement claims.

What happens if I do not reconcile? Unreconciled transactions are chased
weekly. Persistent non-reconciliation results in card suspension.
