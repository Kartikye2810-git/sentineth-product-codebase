# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Owner: Delphine Okonkwo, Director of People Operations
Version: 2.3
Effective date: 6 January 2026

## 1. Working patterns

Sentineth operates a hybrid model. Employees assigned to a hub location are
expected in the office a minimum of two days per week, with Wednesday
designated as the common anchor day across all teams. The second day is
chosen by the team and recorded in the team charter.

Fully remote contracts exist for roles explicitly designated as remote in the
job requisition. A hybrid employee cannot unilaterally convert to a fully
remote contract; the change requires a role redesignation approved by the
department head and People Operations.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's contracted time
zone. Meetings should be scheduled within core hours wherever possible.
Outside core hours, employees are not expected to be responsive.

## 2. Hub locations

The company maintains hub offices in London, Bengaluru, and Lisbon. Desk
capacity is allocated at a ratio of one desk per one point six hybrid
employees, and desks are booked through the workplace system no more than ten
working days in advance.

An employee whose home address is more than fifty miles from their assigned
hub is automatically treated as remote for attendance purposes, but retains
access to the hub and may claim travel to any mandated on-site event.

## 3. Working from another country

Working from a country other than the contracted country of employment is
permitted for a maximum of twenty working days per calendar year, subject to
prior approval from People Operations and Finance. Approval is required
because unplanned presence can create a permanent establishment risk for the
company and a personal tax liability for the employee.

Requests must be submitted at least fifteen working days before travel.
Approval will not be granted for countries on the company's restricted list,
which is maintained by the General Counsel and currently reflects sanctions
exposure and data transfer restrictions rather than any judgement about the
country itself.

Under no circumstances may Restricted information be accessed from a country
outside the approved processing locations listed in the Information Security
Policy.

## 4. Equipment and allowances

Every employee receives a laptop, an external monitor, a keyboard, and a
mouse. Remote and hybrid employees additionally receive a one-off home office
allowance of GBP 600, claimable within the first ninety days of employment
and refreshable every three years.

A monthly connectivity allowance of GBP 30 is paid to fully remote employees
only. Hybrid employees are not eligible for the connectivity allowance
because the hub provides a working location.

Equipment remains company property. On termination, equipment must be
returned within ten working days using the prepaid courier label issued by IT.
Unreturned equipment is charged at depreciated book value through the final
salary payment where the employment contract permits.

## 5. Meeting practice

Any meeting with at least one remote participant is treated as a remote
meeting: all participants join individually from their own device, cameras on
by default, rather than several people sharing a room microphone. This rule
exists to prevent a two-tier meeting where in-room participants dominate.

Meetings longer than thirty minutes require a written agenda circulated at
least two hours in advance. Recurring meetings without an agenda are
cancelled automatically by the calendar hygiene job after three consecutive
occurrences.

Decisions taken in a meeting must be written into the relevant decision log
within one working day. A decision that exists only in a recording is treated
as not yet taken.

## 6. Onboarding of remote employees

Remote employees attend an in-person onboarding week at their nearest hub
within their first sixty days. Travel and accommodation for this week are
arranged centrally and are not charged to the employee's department budget.

Every new remote employee is assigned an onboarding partner from a different
team, who holds a thirty-minute check-in weekly for the first eight weeks.

## 7. Performance and progression

Remote and hybrid employees are assessed against identical criteria. Office
attendance is not an input to performance assessment and must not be
referenced in review documentation. People Operations audits a sample of
review documents each cycle for compliance with this rule.

## 8. Review

This policy is reviewed annually, or sooner if occupancy patterns change
materially. Proposed changes are consulted on with the Employee Forum for a
minimum of fifteen working days before adoption.
